# SAGE quality rules

A document is publishable only when all of these conditions are true:

1. The article has a clear thesis and intended audience.
2. Externally verifiable claims have evidence provenance.
3. Technical claims have passed independent technical review.
4. Editorial review passes without critical findings.
5. Adversarial review has no critical findings.
6. Citation verification passes.
7. The SEO contract is completely satisfied.
8. The production build and link validation pass in CI.
9. Product claims are traceable to approved product facts.
10. Publication occurs through a pull request; autonomous direct writes to `main` are prohibited.
